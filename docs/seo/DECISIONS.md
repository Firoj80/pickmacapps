# Decisions

## 2026-10-18 — Site is early-stage; fix crawl basics before title polish

Chose to treat PickMacApps as early/growth, not mature. About 110 URLs, no Search Console, and only two guides. Coverage of the Mac-app topic is still thin.

Rejected rewriting titles first. There was no sitemap, no canonical, and no baseline, so a title change could not be measured. Crawl and honesty fixes came first.

## 2026-10-18 — Do not disavow links or chase click-through yet

No backlink tool and no ranking data. A disavow, or a title rewrite for clicks, would be a guess. Revisit only after Search Console is connected.

## 2026-10-18 — Fixed the audit, did not submit Search Console

Shipped robots.txt, a sitemap, canonicals, real 404s, cleaned snippets, removed the fake “Added” dates, labeled Editor’s Choice as paid, and removed unverified submit-page claims. Did not invent new categories. Search Console submission stays with the site owner once a public domain is verified.

## 2026-10-19 — Keyword targets, no volume numbers

Search Console is still not connected, so there is no near-miss query list and no keyword-tool volume or difficulty score. Chose not to invent either.

Inferred from TryMacApps’ sitemap and live titles, plus a sample of current Google results: do not lead with “best Mac apps” or “best free Mac apps” (those results are large publishers). Lead with “best {app} alternative” pages where PickMacApps already has the alternative hub and the apps, because those results are mostly other small Mac sites. Do not write Raycast, Alfred, Notion, or Obsidian guides until those apps are actually in the directory.

## Open flags

- Listing dates were shuffled on 2026-10-16 and are no longer shown. Do not put them back as if they were real listing dates.
- New categories (photo, video, AI, security, browsers) still need real apps. Do not publish empty hubs.
- Keyword volumes and difficulty scores are unknown until Search Console or a keyword tool is connected.
- 2026-10-25: Next seven guides are Bartender, Wispr Flow, CleanShot X, Screen Studio, Paste, NotchNook, then AppCleaner. Each is a blog article, not a second copy of the matching alternatives hub. Titles may say “best” only if the first paragraph names who it is best for. Do not publish Raycast, Alfred, Notion, Obsidian, or “best Mac apps” until those apps are in the directory.
